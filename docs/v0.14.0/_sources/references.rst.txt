References
==========

The analytical formulation and notation used throughout PyFiberModes follow
standard guided-wave treatments, including:

* Jacques Bures, *Guided Optics: Optical Fibers and All-Fiber Components*.
* A. W. Snyder and J. D. Love, *Optical Waveguide Theory*.
* D. Gloge, "Weakly guiding fibers," *Applied Optics* 10, 2252–2258 (1971).

For reproducible research, cite the archived PyFiberModes release identified
by the repository's ``CITATION.cff`` file.
